Rules · 1 of 2 · What we flag

Two findings, and a separate list for bad data

Equal standing. Notification and repair are not ranked.

A reason on every flag. The coordinator sees why, not only which.

Same list for both checks. The affected list is read once.

Recall Campaign Tracker
  • Three lanes. Blue and amber are findings. Purple is data problems, always on its own list.
  • Notification: the pair is on the affected list but not in the notices file. That is the whole rule.
  • Repair: the earliest notice starts the clock, the earliest repair on or after it stops it. A repair before the notice does not count.
  • With no repair yet we count to the check date. A vehicle is flagged only when that count passes the limit.
  • Changing the limit on screen recalculates and keeps the choice. At 60 days there are 270 repair flags in our sample, at 74 there are 215, at 90 there are 182.

Limitations · 1 of 2

What the tracker does not do

By design

  • Never contacts an owner
  • Never edits or deletes a source record
  • Never decides for the coordinator
  • One dealership, three CSV files, no live feed
  • Out of scope: returns, resales, other dealers, current owner, central-body status

What it cannot know

  • "Notified" means the notice is on file. It does not know if a letter arrived or came back.
  • "Affected" means on your list. It does not check model or build date.
  • The CSVs are only as good as the export. Bad rows are listed, not guessed.

All numbers today come from synthetic data that we generated, with mistakes planted on purpose.

Recall Campaign Tracker
  • Be direct: this is what it will not do, and most of it is on purpose.
  • The second card matters most. Notified means a notice row exists. Affected means the vehicle is on the list you give us.
  • The affected point leads into the next slide: you asked for a check that uses model and build date.
Limitations · 2 of 2 · Your request

“Repair on an unaffected vehicle”: what we can do today

Already in the tracker

A repair or notice for a vehicle that is not on the affected list is flagged as a data problem with the reason "VIN … is not listed as affected by campaign …". The vehicle page shows In affected list: No and no day count. 3 rows in the sample run.

Not possible yet

Your definition uses model and build date inside the campaign start and end, both included. Our three files have none of these.

Needed: model and build date for each vehicle, and model and dates for each campaign.

To decide with you

  • Is the affected list still the source, or should model and build date replace it?
  • Where will model, build dates and campaign dates come from?
  • If the list and the rule disagree, which one wins?
  • Repairs only, or notices too?
  • A third finding, or a data-quality flag?
